Content sources & preview notes

Reviewed on 22 November 2026. This is a separate local redesign, not a published replacement for cnalab.dev.

Sources and editorial choices

  • CNALab’s live website supplies the lab affiliation, research descriptions, team names and roles, publications, software links, funding information, and contact details.
  • The supplied full-page mockup defines the layout, navy/teal palette, restrained typography, pipeline, resource highlight, people, and recruitment sections. Its placeholders and unsupported claims have been replaced or omitted.
  • The hero uses the original snapshot1.png located alongside the newly supplied mockup in Downloads. It matches the reconstruction in the reference. Its black background is retained and its display saturation is slightly reduced through CSS; the original file is unchanged. No generated scientific imagery is used. No axon count, voxel size, specimen identity, or volume dimensions are asserted.
  • The original headline is retained. “From brain microgeometry to diffusion MRI” appears in the introductory text to clarify the research connection.
  • Three featured papers represent diffusion modeling (2025), deep-learning segmentation (2021), and axonal morphometry (2019). The expandable publication list contains all seven DOI-linked journal papers from the live site, ordered by year. Two 2024 conference entries lack linked records and are omitted. This is a selection, not a claim of a complete bibliography.
  • Titles, years, journals, and DOI links were checked against the live lab page. The Nature publisher page was inaccessible to the retrieval tool; not all publisher destinations were independently verified.
  • The pipeline is an editorial overview, not a claim that every stage is one released, integrated system. Synthetic tissue is labeled a research direction. Unsupported X-ray nanoholotomography and universal automatic tracing claims were omitted.
  • Scattering to diffusion is highlighted as a featured method, rather than asserting a lab-designated flagship. The code repository was independently opened. Invented numerical metrics were replaced with paper, code, and data links.
  • All five people and their roles match the live site. The two available real portraits are used. Three members are presented in a text roster rather than using the live site’s generic portrait placeholder. No unverified qualifications or vacancies were added.
  • All six software repositories are linked. gACSON is credited through its Andrea Behanova repository; SproutAngio through the Mustafa Beter repository. Software licenses and language tags are not inferred.
  • The scattering dataset link is retained. Three EM dataset descriptions on the live site share one DOI (10.23729/bad417ca-553f-4fa6-ae0a-22eddd29a230). They are consolidated into one neutrally labeled repository link until separate identifiers are confirmed. The dataset viewer requires JavaScript; record contents and download availability were not independently verified.
  • No dedicated news feed was found. “Research updates” includes the verified 2025 paper and the 2024 grant described in the PI biography. Only years are shown; dates were not invented.
  • The recruitment section provides a real email contact. The live site advertises hiring but gives no vacancy title, deadline, or application link, so no specific job offer is presented.

Before publication

Confirm that the team roster is still current, resolve the duplicate dataset links, and confirm image reuse permissions and any desired scientific caption metadata. The current site and domain have not been changed.

Preview and portability

Open index.html directly, or serve this directory with any static web server. No build step, account, backend, or external font service is required. All internal files use relative paths, including for GitHub Pages project sites. No custom domain or automatic deployment configuration is included.
